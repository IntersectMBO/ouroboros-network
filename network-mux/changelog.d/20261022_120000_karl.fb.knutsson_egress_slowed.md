### Breaking

- The egress counters count, per tier, the bearers whose batches have lately
  taken at least 2, 4, 8 and 16 times their own time at the socket, waiting
  for tokens the rest (`TierCounts.tcSlowed`): what the budget's order costs
  each bearer, whatever its demand. `bsServed` holds each bearer's standing,
  and `awaitGrantWaited` and `awaitGrantCharged` take the previous batch's
  write time beside the wait at the writability gate.
