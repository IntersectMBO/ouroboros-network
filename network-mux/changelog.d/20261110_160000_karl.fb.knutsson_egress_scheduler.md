### Breaking

- `Network.Mux.Egress.muxer` takes the lane it serves (`LaneEgress`):
  unscheduled, the direct lane on the budget's credit, the reserved slice, or
  the scheduled lane, which takes a grant from the budget for every batch and
  reports what the batch carried, bytes per mini-protocol and the granted
  rank, to a `ChargeSink`.
- `Bearer` gains `awaitWritable`, the wait at the socket's writability gate
  before a scheduled batch asks for its grant.
- `MiniProtocolState` carries the protocol's `EgressVar`, an `EgressState` of
  the batches held and the bytes sent; `ByteSlots` are those variables and
  `withByteCells` takes them, so the sent count rides the release the muxer
  makes anyway. `Wanton` is a record of its bytes and that variable,
  `newWanton` takes the variable. A mini-protocol completes, on every mux,
  once its last batch is written, not once the muxer has taken it.
- `withCounters` and `countersLoop` take the budget's buckets; `CountersTrace`
  gains `TraceEgressCounters` and `TraceEgressBurst`; `Failures` counts the
  writability gate's timeouts (`mfGateTimeouts`), per lane that timed out.
- `Trace` gains `TraceEgressRank`, the rank a mux's rule first answers, and
  `BearerTrace` gains `TraceEgressGrant`, a scheduled batch's grant with its
  waits at the gate and for tokens.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `newWithEgress` and `EgressPolicy`: a mux whose egress the node's
  budget schedules, one queue and one muxer per `Lane` in use (`Direct`,
  `Slice`, `Scheduled`). The lane rule is the policy's; `directionSplit`
  sends what we serve to the scheduled lane and our own requests direct.
  Without a slice bucket the rule's `Slice` traffic is scheduled.
- Added `Network.Mux.Egress.Bucket`: a token bucket shared by every bearer of
  the process. Bearers register and are served in order of `Rank`, lower
  first, FIFO among equals. With a `Rotation` the rank is a `Tier` (local
  roots, partners, pools, strangers, the rest), the place within a tier is
  dealt at random and re-dealt every period, and the local roots and the
  tiers from `roTurnsFrom` up take turns instead. `setEgressRank` fixes a
  mux's rank; `setEgressRankSource` installs a rule asked whenever its bearer
  queues.
- Added `Network.Mux.Egress.Floor`, attached with `attachFloor`: a small share
  of the budget for the credited bearers the strict order does not reach,
  shared fairly in bytes across three classes and in turns within a class,
  charged to the budget on credit.
- Added `Network.Mux.Egress.Counters` and `Network.Mux.Egress.Burst`:
  `SchedulingCounts`, the bytes and batches per lane, the waits for tokens and
  at the writability gate, the ranked queue's and the slice's apart, the
  budget's level and queue, and per tier (`TierCounts`) the bytes, batches,
  queued, served and the bearers the order slows; and each `Burst` of
  credited demand on the budget, traced as it closes. The counters loop
  traces them every interval beside the mux counters.
- Added `makeSocketBearerWith` and `tcpNotSentLowWat` to `Network.Mux.Bearer`:
  a socket bearer with TCP_NOTSENT_LOWAT set, the option by platform.
- Added `mux-bucket-demo`.
