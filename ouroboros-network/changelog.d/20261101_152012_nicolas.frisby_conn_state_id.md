### Breaking

- `ExpandedInitiatorContext` gains `eicConnStateId`, the connection's
  `ConnStateId`, which unlike its `ConnectionId` is never reused.
- `Connected` carries the connection's `ConnStateId`.
- `ConnStateId` moved to `Ouroboros.Network.ConnectionId`.
  `Ouroboros.Network.ConnectionManager.State` and `Ouroboros.Network.Context`
  re-export it.
