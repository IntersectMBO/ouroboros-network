### Breaking

- The inbound tx-submission (V2) server parks straight after a decision
  with nothing to do.  It used to decide once more when the peer had
  just come from a request or a submission, in case the idle peer could
  claim what the busy one could not; nothing in a decision reads that
  phase, so the second decision could only repeat the first.
  `PeerTxLocalState` loses `peerPhase`, and `PeerPhase` is gone.

### Non-Breaking

- `ouroboros-network:ouroboros-network-tests-lib`:
  `Test.Ouroboros.Network.TxSubmission.Types` exports
  `blockingTxIdRequest`, the continuation of a server state that is a
  blocking txid request, for a test that answers it by hand.
