### Breaking

- Inbound tx-submission (V2) tells the slow tier's txids apart and
  counts the peers on each tier: the counters gain `txIdsReceivedSlow`
  (the ids carried by replies to requests sent on the slow tier) and
  the counters thread emits a `TraceTxTiers` census, the peers served
  fast by standing, by the coin, as tx sources, and the slow ones, as
  four gauges `txSubmission.tiers.{fastByRoot,fastByCoin,fastBySource,slow}`.
