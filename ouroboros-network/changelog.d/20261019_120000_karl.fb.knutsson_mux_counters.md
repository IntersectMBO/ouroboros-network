### Breaking

- `makeConnectionHandler` takes the node-wide `Mx.MuxCounters` every mux it
  creates is counted in, or `Nothing`.
- `Diffusion.Configuration` has `dcMuxCounters` and `dcLocalMuxCounters`,
  the counters of the node-to-node and the node-to-client muxes, if the node
  keeps any, and `Diffusion.Tracers` has `dtMuxCountersTracer`, on which
  diffusion traces them every `dcMuxCountersInterval`.

### Non-Breaking

- `Network.Mux.Tracing` renders `Mx.CountersTrace`, with metrics `egress.*`,
  `ingress.*`, `peerClosed`, `otherErrors` and `miniProtocols.*`, and the same
  under
  `local*`; the bytes as `.bytes` and `.bytes.<num>.<initiator|responder>`
  under egress and ingress.
