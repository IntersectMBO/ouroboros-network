### Breaking

- Removed `retainedExpiredKeys`; `retainedExpireUpTo` is the bounded form.
- `sweepSharedState` no longer takes the peers' references: a lookup entry
  lives with its residence and goes with it, and `TxEntry` counts its
  advertisers (`txAdvertisers`), kept by the peers' own writes, so an active
  entry nobody advertises, leases, attempts or submits is dropped as
  abandoned.  `PeerTxInFlight` loses `pifAcksPending`, which nothing read.
- `handleSubmittedTxs` and the peer API's `applySubmittedTxs` take the
  accepted txids with their keys: a tx accepted under a key whose
  residence ended while the submission ran is retained afresh under a new
  key.
- `PeerTxRegistry` holds the peers' counters cells alone.

### Non-Breaking

- Fixed the V2 tx-submission sweep stalling under load. One sweep expired
  every retained transaction past its deadline in a single STM transaction;
  once that transaction ran longer than the gap between peers' writes it
  never committed, nothing expired, and each retry had more to do. The
  counters thread spun in it and stopped emitting counters. Each sweep now
  expires at most 1000 entries, earliest first, examines as many entries of
  the tx table from where the last sweep stopped, and while expired entries
  remain goes on in further transactions of that size, 10 ms apart. A
  sweep reads nothing but the state, and never drops an entry a peer still
  advertises; acknowledging txids the node already has writes nothing
  shared, as before.
- A body arriving after its key's residence ended counts as late only, not
  as omitted too; a submission completing after another peer retained the
  tx and that retention expired retains it afresh under a new key, never
  under the dead one, unless the txid is interned again meanwhile: active
  under another key it is left to that entry, retained again its retention
  is extended; every write of the shared state advances `sharedRevision`.
- Added `retainedExpireUpTo`, which expires at most a given number of
  entries (none for a limit of zero or less), `retainedDueBy`,
  `sweepSharedStateUpTo`, the bounded sweep, which takes and returns a
  `SweepCursor` and reports whether expired entries remain, whatever its
  limit allowed, and
  `expireSharedStateUpTo`, expiry alone, with which the counters thread
  drains a backlog; `sweepInterval`, `sweepBatch` and `drainPause` are the
  thread's constants.
- `TraceSharedTxState` carries `sharedRevision`, whose growth between two
  traces is the rate of writes to the shared state.
