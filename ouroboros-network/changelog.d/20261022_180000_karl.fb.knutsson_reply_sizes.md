### Breaking

- The inbound tx-submission (V2) counters gain five reply-size counts,
  `txIdReplies0`, `txIdReplies1`, `txIdReplies2to4`, `txIdReplies5to9`
  and `txIdReplies10`, a partition of the replies to requests for txids by
  the number a reply carried, published as metrics; a reply to a request
  for acknowledgements alone, empty by construction, bumps none.
