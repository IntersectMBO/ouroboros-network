### Breaking

- `RetainedTxs` keys its deadlines on `Word64` nanoseconds of monotonic time
  instead of `Time`: `retainedSingleton`, `retainedFromList`,
  `retainedToList`, `retainedLookup`, `retainedInsertMax`,
  `retainedExpireUpTo` and `retainedDueBy` take or return `Word64`.
  Convert a deadline with `retainDeadline` (rounds up) and the current time
  with `retainNow` (rounds down), so an entry never expires before its
  deadline; a negative time is due at once, a time past the range at its
  end.
- In the `TraceSharedTxState` JSON, the `sharedRetainedTxs` deadlines are
  numbers, in seconds, where they were strings.

### Non-Breaking

- The retention queue compares machine words instead of `Integer`
  picoseconds; the comparisons were a fifth of the sweep's time under load.
