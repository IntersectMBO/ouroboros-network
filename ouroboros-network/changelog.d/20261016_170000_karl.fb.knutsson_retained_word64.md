### Breaking

- `RetainedTxs` keys its deadlines on `RetainTime`, a newtype over `Word64`
  nanoseconds of monotonic time, instead of `Time`: `retainedSingleton`,
  `retainedFromList`, `retainedToList`, `retainedLookup`,
  `retainedInsertMax`, `retainedExpireUpTo` and `retainedDueBy` take or
  return `RetainTime`.  It is only compared, never computed with: make a
  deadline with `retainDeadline` (rounds up) and the current time with
  `retainNow` (rounds down), so an entry never expires before its deadline;
  a negative time is due at once, a time past the range at its end.
  `retainTimeSeconds` reads one as seconds, for traces.
- In the `TraceSharedTxState` JSON, the `sharedRetainedTxs` deadlines are
  numbers, in seconds, where they were strings.

### Non-Breaking

- The retention queue compares machine words instead of `Integer`
  picoseconds; the comparisons were a fifth of the sweep's time under load.
