### Breaking

- A slow peer whose gate holds, with no body buffered and no advertised
  key with a live entry, parks on its gate's timer alone (`NoCounter`): a
  park on the claimable counter would wake it for every entry becoming
  claimable or lease released, for a decision that could act on nothing,
  since the keys it holds stay dead until it acknowledges them, after the
  gate.  `ResolvedDoNothing` carries a zero for such a park, and
  `awaitMirrorChange` takes the mirror as a `Maybe`, none for a park on
  the delay alone.
