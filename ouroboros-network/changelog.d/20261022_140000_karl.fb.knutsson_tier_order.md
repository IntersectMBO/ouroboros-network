### Breaking

- The inbound tx-submission (V2) fast tier is the first `txFastPeers` of
  the inbound peers in a salted order, the salt drawn afresh every
  `txTierRotation`, as the peer-sharing responder orders its answer: a
  peer leaving a fast slot hands it to the next in the order at once, and
  an arrival takes its place.  A peer enters the order once its server's
  initial delay has passed, before its first decision, so a peer not
  polling yet holds no fast slot: the server calls the peer API's
  `joinTiers` once.  `withPeer`, `rotateTiers` and `txCountersThreadV2`
  require `Hashable peeraddr`; `registerPeerTier` only makes the entry,
  `joinTier` and `leaveTier` move a peer in and out of the order.
