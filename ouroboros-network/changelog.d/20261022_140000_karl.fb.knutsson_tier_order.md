### Breaking

- The inbound tx-submission (V2) fast tier is the first `txFastPeers` of
  the inbound peers in a salted order, the salt drawn afresh every
  `txTierRotation`, as the peer-sharing responder orders its answer: a
  peer leaving a fast slot hands it to the next in the order at once, and
  an arrival takes its place.  A peer enters the order at its first
  decision, after the server's initial delay, so a peer not polling yet
  holds no fast slot.  `withPeer`, `rotateTiers` and
  `txTierRotationThreadV2` require `Hashable peeraddr`; `registerPeerTier`
  only makes the entry, `joinTier` and `leaveTier` move a peer in and out
  of the order.
