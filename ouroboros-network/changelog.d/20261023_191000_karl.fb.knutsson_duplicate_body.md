- The inbound tx-submission (V2) server rejects a `MsgReplyTxs` that
  carries a txid twice as `ProtocolErrorTxNotRequested`, traced like the
  other body errors.  The second copy answered no pending key and passed
  for a late body, which cancelled the omission of a key the peer had left
  out of the same reply.
- `ouroboros-network:ouroboros-network-tests-lib`: `Impairment` gains
  `impairDuplicateBody`, which sends the first body of each non-empty
  reply twice; the generator draws it among the other impairments.
