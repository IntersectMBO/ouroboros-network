### Breaking

- Inbound tx-submission (V2) polls its peers for txids in two tiers.
  Local roots, tx sources (peers that, while served slow, delivered
  `txSourceRate` novel accepted txs per second over `txSourceWindow`) and
  `txFastPeers` inbound peers drawn at random every `txTierRotation` are
  polled as before; every other peer waits `txSlowCadence`, jittered by
  `txSlowJitter`, between txid requests, blocking ones included, and parks
  meanwhile on a counter that moves when an entry becomes claimable, not
  on the shared generation, unless it holds a body it could not submit.
  Promotion is upward only.  `TxDecisionPolicy` carries the six knobs;
  `txCountersThreadV2` throws `InvalidTxDecisionPolicy` at start on a
  policy that fails `saneTxDecisionPolicy`; the counters gain
  `txIdSlowReqsSent`; the shared state's trace gains the gauge
  `txSubmission.sharedState.claimable`.
- `PeerTxLocalState` gains `peerTier`, `peerRequestTiers`, `peerSlowGate`,
  `peerNovelAt` and `peerJitterSeed`; `SharedTxState` gains
  `sharedClaimable`, the count of claimable entries, which the registry
  mirrors in its own `registryClaimable` so a parked peer wakes when that
  count moves and not on every write of the shared state; `PeerTxAPI`
  gains `awaitClaimableChange` and `apiJitterSeed`; `PeerTxRegistry` gains
  `peerTiers`, `registrySalt`, `registryRng` and `registryClaimable`, and
  `newPeerTxRegistry` takes the coin's generator; `PeerTier`,
  `TierStanding`, `PeerTierState` and `PeerTierEntry` are new; `withTier`
  takes the policy and the registry and places the order again when a
  peer's root standing changes; `withPeer` takes whether the peer is a
  local root; the counters thread `txCountersThreadV2` runs the coin's
  rotation too.
- `PublicPeerSelectionState` is a record with `localRootAddrs` beside
  `availableToShare`, both strict and with a `NoThunks` instance, so the
  responder can ask whether a peer is a local root: one whose connection
  comes from its listening address, as an initiator-and-responder node's
  does.
