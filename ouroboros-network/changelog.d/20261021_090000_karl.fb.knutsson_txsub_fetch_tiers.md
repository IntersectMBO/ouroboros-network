### Breaking

- Inbound tx-submission (V2) polls its peers for txids in two tiers.
  Local roots, tx sources (`txSourceRate` novel accepted txs per second
  over `txSourceWindow`) and `txFastPeers` inbound peers drawn at random
  every `txTierRotation` are polled as before; every other peer waits
  `txSlowCadence`, jittered by `txSlowJitter`, between txid requests,
  blocking ones included, and parks meanwhile on a counter that moves when
  an entry becomes claimable, not on the shared generation, unless it
  holds a body it could not submit.  Promotion is upward only.  `TxDecisionPolicy` carries the five knobs;
  `newPeerTxRegistry` takes the coin's generator; `withPeer` takes whether
  the peer is a local root; `txTierRotationThreadV2` runs the coin; the
  counters gain `txIdSlowReqsSent`.  `PublicPeerSelectionState` is a record
  with `localRootAddrs` beside `availableToShare`, so the responder can ask
  whether a peer is a local root.
