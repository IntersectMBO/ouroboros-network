### Breaking

- Inbound tx-submission (V2) polls its peers for txids in two tiers.
  Local roots, tx sources (peers that, while served slow, delivered
  `txSourceCount` novel accepted txs inside `txSourceWindow`) and
  `txFastPeers` inbound peers drawn at random every `txTierRotation` are
  polled as before; every other peer waits `txSlowCadence`, jittered by
  `txSlowJitter`, between txid requests, blocking ones included, and parks
  meanwhile on a counter that moves when an entry becomes claimable, not
  on the shared generation, unless it holds a body it could not submit;
  acknowledgements that free a buffered body go out meanwhile, in a
  request for no txids.
  Promotion is upward only.  `TxDecisionPolicy` carries the six knobs,
  bounded by `saneTxDecisionPolicy` (a `TxConfigurationError` per bound,
  among them a source window over an hour, `maxSourceWindow`);
  `txCountersThreadV2` takes the `TxSubmissionConfig` and throws the
  error at start on a policy and config that fail it; the counters gain
  `txIdSlowReqsSent`, the requests for txids sent on the slow tier, and
  `txIdAckOnlySent`, the requests for acknowledgements alone; the shared
  state's trace gains the gauge
  `txSubmission.sharedState.claimable`.
- `PeerTxLocalState` gains `peerTier`, `peerTxIdRequests` (the outstanding
  txid requests as their replies are counted, each a `TxIdRequest`: for
  txids on a tier, or for acknowledgements alone), `peerSlowGate`,
  `peerNovelAt` (one entry per accepting submission inside the source
  window) and `peerJitterSeed`; `SharedTxState` gains
  `sharedClaimable`, a counter that moves whenever an entry becomes
  claimable (the count of claimable entries is `claimableTxCount`); the
  registry mirrors it and `sharedGeneration` in variables of its own
  (`registryMirrors`, a `CounterMirrors`) so a parked peer wakes when the
  counter it parks on moves and not on every write of the shared state;
  `PeerTxAPI` gains `apiJitterSeed`;
  `PeerTxRegistry` gains `peerTiers`, `registrySalt`, `registryRng` and
  `registryMirrors`, and `newPeerTxRegistry` takes the coin's generator;
  `PeerTier`, `TierStanding`, `PeerTierState` and `PeerTierEntry` are new;
  `withTier` takes the policy and runs in the decision's transaction;
  `newPeerTxRegistry` takes whether a peer is a local root, which a join
  asks for the joining peer and the counters thread `txCountersThreadV2`,
  which runs the coin's rotation too, asks once a sweep for every peer in
  the order (`refreshTierRoots`), placing the order again on a change,
  each in a transaction of its own, so that no decision reads the
  governor's state.
- `PublicPeerSelectionState` is a record with `localRootAddrs` beside
  `availableToShare`, both strict and with a `NoThunks` instance, so the
  responder can ask whether a peer is a local root: one whose connection
  comes from its listening address, as an initiator-and-responder node's
  does.
- `ouroboros-network:ouroboros-network-tests-lib`: the test node's
  `NodeArguments` gain `aTxSubmissionConfig`, its `newNodeKernel` takes
  the seed of the registry's coin and the registry asks the node's public
  state for the local roots; `PeerAddr` has a `NoThunks` instance;
  `Test.Ouroboros.Network.TxSubmission.SizedBytes` exports `messageSize`,
  `isReplyTxs` and `isRequestTxs`.
