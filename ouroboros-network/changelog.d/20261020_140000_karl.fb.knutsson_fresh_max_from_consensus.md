### Breaking

- The egress credit buckets are sized by what consensus says one
  announcement can carry under the chain's current parameters:
  `daFreshMaxBytes` (block body plus, where the chain has Leios, an
  endorser block and its closure at their maxima), read at every use so a
  parameter change takes effect at once; and the fresh-bytes count
  `daFreshBytes` is required. `esFreshMaxBytes`, `esFreshBytesPerSecond`
  and the clock that stood in for the count are gone; `newPoolAllowances`
  takes the allowance as a reading. The maxima were hard-coded for a
  1 MiB closure, four times this chain's.
