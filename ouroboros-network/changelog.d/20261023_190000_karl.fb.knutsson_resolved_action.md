### Breaking

- The inbound tx-submission (V2) registry resolves a decision in the
  transaction that makes it: `runNextPeerAction` and
  `runNextPeerActionPipelined` return a `ResolvedPeerAction`, the action
  with the txids and sizes to request or the bodies to submit, and
  `resolveTxRequest` and `resolveBufferedTxs` are gone from `PeerTxAPI`.
  Before, the keys were resolved in a later read, and a key whose tx
  another peer had submitted meanwhile and whose retention had expired
  failed the resolution and the peer.
