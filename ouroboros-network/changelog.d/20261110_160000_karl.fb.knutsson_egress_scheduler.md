### Breaking

- `DiffusionConfiguration` gains `dcEgressScheduling :: Maybe EgressScheduling`;
  `DiffusionArguments` gains `daEgressPoolKey`, `daFreshBytes` and
  `daFreshMaxBytes`; `DiffusionTracers` gains `dtPoolRelaysTracer`.
- `PublicPeerSelectionState` is a record, `hotUpstreamSince` beside
  `availableToShare`; `PeerSelectionState` keeps `activeSince`, when each
  active peer was promoted.
- The connection handler takes scheduled egress as one `Maybe`: the node's
  egress policy with the `EgressPolicyRule` asked per connection as its
  bearer queues, which also gives the sink its scheduled bytes are reported
  to.
- `DNSActions` gains `dnsLookupSRV` and `dnsLookupIPs`, and `DNSPeersKind`
  the constructor `DNSPoolRelay`, for the pool relays resolver.

### Non-Breaking

- Scheduled egress for node-to-node connections, when `dcEgressScheduling` is
  given: every connection's mux shares one budget bucket of `esBudget` bytes
  per second, a slice of `esSlicePercent` reserved for the lane the rule
  names, a floor of `esFloorPercent` for the credited tiers, and the order
  within a tier re-dealt every `esRotationPeriod`; `esNotSentLowWat` sets
  TCP_NOTSENT_LOWAT on every node-to-node socket. `defaultEgressSchedulingWith`
  is 250 Mb/s, a bucket of two batches, a 15% slice, a 10% floor and 599 s.
- A peer's tier is its standing, read as its bearer joins the queue: the local
  roots first, then the partners, duplex peers hot both ways for
  `esTenureThreshold`, then the credited tiers, where a big pool's relays share
  the pool's credit bucket and a stranger has its own, opened after
  `esStrangerLock`, then the rest. Added `Ouroboros.Network.Diffusion.PoolAllowances`,
  the credit buckets by pool, a pool known by its relay list, refilled by the
  bytes the chain freshly announces (`daFreshBytes`) and sized by the most
  one announcement can carry (`daFreshMaxBytes`), both read from consensus;
  and `Ouroboros.Network.Diffusion.PoolRelays`, the thread that resolves
  every big pool's relays on two workers of its own and keeps the buckets'
  address index in step with the ledger, traced as `TracePoolRelays`.
- The mux counters tracing carries the egress counters under `Egress` and each
  burst under `Burst`, with metrics `egress.direct.bytes`, `egress.slice.*`,
  `egress.scheduled.*`, `egress.budget.*` and `egress.tier.<tier>.*` (bytes,
  batches, queued, served, `slowed.<n>x`), and `mux.egress.gateTimeouts`; the
  mux tracer gains `EgressGrant` and `EgressRank`.
- Added `srvTargetsWithTTL` and `addressLookupsWithTTL`, the lookups behind
  the new `DNSActions` fields.
