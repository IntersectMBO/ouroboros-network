### Non-Breaking

- The testnet simulation's nodes keep node-to-node mux counters, at the
  default interval or the one `diffusionSimulationWithCounters` gives, traced
  as `DiffusionMuxCountersTrace`; a property checks what diffusion traces of
  them: a snapshot every interval, a second at least,
  from the node joining the network, or none at an interval of zero, counts
  that never go down, no protocol error anywhere, and chain-sync bytes
  counted as each chain-sync initiator and responder starts.
