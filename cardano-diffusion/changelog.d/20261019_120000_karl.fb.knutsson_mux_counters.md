### Non-Breaking

- The testnet simulation can give its nodes node-to-node mux counters, with
  `diffusionSimulationWithCounters`, traced as `DiffusionMuxCountersTrace`;
  a property checks what diffusion traces of them: a snapshot every interval
  from the node joining the network, counts that never go down, no protocol
  error anywhere, and chain-sync bytes counted as each chain-sync initiator
  and responder starts.
