### Breaking

- `CardanoConsensusArguments` gains `freshBytes` and `freshMaxBytes`: the bytes
  the chain has freshly announced and the most one announcement can carry,
  both from consensus, which refill and size the egress credit buckets.

### Non-Breaking

- Added `defaultEgressScheduling`: the diffusion defaults with the
  tx-submission initiator in the reserved slice, the tx-submission
  responder's requests direct and keep-alive uncharged. Diffusion keys the
  credit buckets by the peer's address without its port.
- The testnet simulation runs its properties with scheduled egress on and off.
